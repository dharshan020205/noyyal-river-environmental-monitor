# Presentation demonstration script (approximately 4–5 minutes)

## Opening (30 seconds)
"Our project is the Noyyal River Environmental Monitor, an academic prototype focused on water-quality evidence and pollution-abatement decision support in the Tiruppur region. It brings together a small set of published and officially reported observations, cleans them, compares the available measurements, and presents practical actions for further investigation."

## Problem and objective (40 seconds)
"The Noyyal River has faced water-quality pressures associated with industrial effluent and municipal sewage. The objective of this prototype is to make selected reported measurements easier to inspect and interpret, and to connect the evidence to possible monitoring and abatement actions."

## Overview page (45 seconds)
"On the Overview page, the dashboard summarizes the available TDS observations and shows the reported values by location and period. The important point is that the observations come from different years and reporting contexts. We therefore do not treat them as one continuous time series."

## Data explorer (45 seconds)
"The Data Explorer lets us filter by location, reporting period and parameter. The user can inspect the underlying records and download the filtered observations as CSV. This makes the analysis traceable to the input data rather than hiding the values behind a chart."

## Comparative analysis (60 seconds)
"This page displays the two CPCB-referenced 2014–15 samples and compares TSS, TDS, BOD and COD. Each parameter is shown separately because TDS has a much larger numerical magnitude and would otherwise visually dominate the smaller values. The screening messages only describe whether a reported downstream value is higher or lower. They do not prove the source of pollution or establish causation."

## Abatement plan (50 seconds)
"Based on the indicators and the cited context, the prototype lists practical areas for action: industrial effluent treatment, salt and TDS reduction, RO and zero-liquid-discharge performance, municipal sewage management, and monitoring with laboratory verification. These are recommendations for investigation and planning, not engineering designs or legal compliance decisions."

## Methodology and limitations (45 seconds)
"The workflow includes CSV input, validation, numeric cleaning, removal of incomplete or negative measurements, descriptive comparisons and interactive visualization. The dataset is small, so the application does not claim a trained predictive model or long-term trend. TDS alone cannot identify the chemical composition or source of dissolved solids. Current sampling and accredited laboratory confirmation remain essential."

## Closing (20 seconds)
"In summary, the contribution is a transparent, interactive data-exploration and decision-support prototype that connects reported water-quality evidence with actionable areas for further monitoring and pollution abatement."
